Privacy Policy — Paper Billionaire

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This privacy policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and who it is shared with when you use the Paper Billionaire mobile game (published on Google Play as com.paperbillionaire.game).

1. Data we collect

DataWhen collectedPurpose
Anonymous device identifierAutomatically on first launchCloud backup of progress, cross-device sync
Email address (Google linking)Only if you use "Link with Google"Matching your account to your Google identity, recovering progress
Game progress data (level, net worth, inventory, etc.)Automatically while playingCloud backup, leaderboard

We do not collect sensitive data such as location, contacts, photos, SMS, or call logs.

2. Third-party services

  • Supabase — our cloud database/authentication backend, stores game saves.
  • Google AdMob — serves in-app ads, uses the advertising identifier.
  • Google Play Billing (expo-iap) — processes in-app purchases.
  • Google Sign-In — optional account linking.

Each service's own privacy policy also applies.

3. Data storage and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored on Supabase infrastructure. You may contact us to request deletion of your data.

4. Children's privacy

Paper Billionaire is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Your rights

Under applicable laws (e.g. GDPR/KVKK) you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data. Contact us using the email below.
